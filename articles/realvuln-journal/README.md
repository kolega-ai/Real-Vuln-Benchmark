# RealVuln Journal

The narrative record of the RealVuln benchmark — every release, benchmark run, and methodology change, written as a dated journal rather than a blog. Each entry is authored by **Faizan Raza** (co-author of the RealVuln paper, with John Pellew) and narrates *why* we ran a test or cut a release, what we observed, and why we think the results moved the way they did.

The journal is deliberately neutral: it reports measurements and interpretation from the open benchmark data (`github.com/kolega-ai/Real-Vuln-Benchmark`), never vendor endorsements.
