Scanners run locally on the pinned repositories. Participants send normalized finding locations to the private scoring service. The service scores them against withheld ground truth and returns a receipt. The public page receives aggregate scores and receipt metadata. Submitted findings are not retained by the scoring service.

This page is a preview of the receipt flow. External submissions open after the private ground truth has a reviewed standard input identity; the current internal scoring contract uses private categories.

A service-issued receipt confirms how submitted locations scored. It does not prove that a scanner independently produced them or that a full scan completed; run provenance is recorded separately.
